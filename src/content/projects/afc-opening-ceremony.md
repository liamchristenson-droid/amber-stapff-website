working_under: Rizer
overview: My involvement began during the proposal phase, where I designed the
  pitch deck that helped secure the project. Following the successful bid, I
  continued working on the event, designing large-scale visual elements for both
  the opening and closing ceremonies.
challenge: The project required translating the creative vision for a major
  international sporting event into impactful physical elements within a stadium
  environment. Designs needed to work at monumental scale, be clearly visible to
  both live audiences and broadcast viewers, and align with the tournament’s
  branding.
approach: For the opening ceremony, I designed the large-scale ground cloth, a
  central visual feature within the stadium performance space, along with the
  Dream Flags, which formed part of the choreographed performance elements. For
  the closing ceremony, I designed the trophy presentation stage, ensuring the
  final celebratory moment of the tournament had a visually striking and
  broadcast-ready backdrop for the winning team and official presentations.
outcome: The final designs were successfully delivered as part of the
  tournament’s opening ceremony, contributing to memorable visual moments for
  both stadium audiences and international broadcast. From the initial pitch
  deck through to the final elements, the work helped bring the creative vision
  to life and supported the delivery of a polished and celebratory conclusion to
  the tournament.
