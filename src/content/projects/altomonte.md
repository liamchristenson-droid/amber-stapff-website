overview: Altomonte is a fresh and purpose-driven brand in the fruit and
  vegetable sector, built on a commitment to local Victorian farmers and
  transparent sourcing. Its pledge to return a portion of profits to growers
  through a formal initiative with the Victorian Farmers Federation required a
  brand identity that felt equally authentic, contemporary and values-led.
challenge: To build a new brand that balanced freshness and trust with purpose
  and advocacy. The design system needed to feel clean and modern while
  connecting emotionally to farming, regional pride and the story behind the
  product.
approach: The logo was designed for flexibility, legibility and strong
  recognition across packaging formats. The identity features a natural yet
  contemporary palette, refined typography and a clear hierarchy for standout
  shelf presence, with packaging focused on communicating freshness, quality and
  transparency.
outcome: The final brand system presents Altomonte as a purpose-led,
  contemporary presence in the fresh produce market — well positioned to grow
  awareness and build trust with consumers and industry partners.
