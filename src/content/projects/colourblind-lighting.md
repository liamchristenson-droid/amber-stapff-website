overview: Colourblind is a leading creative studio specialising in the design
  and control of lighting and video for live music, television and immersive
  installations. With a portfolio featuring artists like G Flip, Glass Beams and
  Crowded House, their work is known for its technical precision. Engaged to
  redesign their website, my role was to create a digital presence that captures
  the energy and scale of their projects while remaining intuitive for potential
  clients.
challenge: "To reflect this duality online: showcasing high-calibre creative
  work in a visually rich, performance-driven site that still functions
  seamlessly across devices. Their portfolio spans festivals, tours and award
  shows, so the site needed to accommodate dynamic visual content without
  sacrificing performance or clarity."
approach: I started with an in-depth exploration of Colourblind's brand. The
  design prioritised spaciousness and clarity, allowing imagery to shine without
  distraction. A dark, minimal palette placed the focus firmly on lighting,
  while crisp typography reinforced precision and professionalism.
outcome: The new website delivers a strong digital identity for Colourblind —
  visually striking, technically robust and easy to update as new projects go
  live.
