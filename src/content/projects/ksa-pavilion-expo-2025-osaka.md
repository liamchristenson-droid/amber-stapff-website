overview: As part of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's presence at Expo 2025 Osaka,
  I worked closely with the THA Staffing & Beyond Limits team to deliver design
  support across a series of branded activations and events. From creative
  concepts to final artwork, I developed event visuals and supporting pitch
  materials aligned with the Pavilion's overarching brand identity, showcasing a
  forward-thinking, culturally rich vision of Saudi Arabia on the world stage.
challenge: Each activation needed to express a distinct theme — from space
  exploration to female empowerment and sustainability — while staying true to
  the Pavilion's broader brand guidelines. The designs had to adapt to different
  formats and locations across the site while maintaining a consistent visual
  tone and message.
approach: I designed visual identities and collateral for key activations
  including Mission to Mars, Her Influence, Wonders of Arabia, Films from Saudi
  Arabia, The Botanist, the Saudi-Japan Expo Investment Forum, the Saudi Vision
  2030 Forum and National Day Celebrations. In parallel, I supported the team
  with high-impact pitch decks and reports across various stages of the project.
outcome: "The work contributed to a cohesive and visually striking presence for
  the Kingdom throughout Expo 2025. From conceptual activations to executive
  communications, each design touchpoint reinforced the Pavilion's ambition: to
  inspire, engage and share Saudi Arabia's evolving story with a global
  audience."
