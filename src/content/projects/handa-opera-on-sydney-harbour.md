intro: Ten seasons of immersive event branding for one of the world's most iconic outdoor stages.
overview: Handa Opera on Sydney Harbour is one of Australia's most celebrated large-scale outdoor events — a spectacular open-air opera experience set on the Sydney waterfront. Since 2015 I have worked as lead designer across every production, delivering immersive event branding. Each year presents a new opera or musical, and a new design opportunity requiring a fresh interpretation of the story, tone and spatial experience through graphics, signage and styling.
challenge: To reinvent the brand identity of a recurring flagship event every season while maintaining consistency with Opera Australia's overarching brand. Each visual identity must reflect the narrative and aesthetic of the chosen opera or musical, respond to an evolving site layout, and work seamlessly across a variety of formats and materials in a challenging outdoor environment.
approach: Every season begins with creative exploration of the opera's narrative, setting and tone. Drawing from these, I design a visual system that translates into large-scale physical graphics, working closely with printers and production teams to bring the concepts to life on site — ensuring every element is impactful, durable and aligned with the overall audience journey.
outcome: From bold classics to Broadway adaptations, my work has helped shape the visual identity of Handa Opera on Sydney Harbour over ten years. The results are immersive, highly branded environments that enhance the audience experience and bring storytelling to life in one of the world's most iconic outdoor venues.
