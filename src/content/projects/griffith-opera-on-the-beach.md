overview: Griffith Opera on the Beach transforms the Gold Coast coastline into a
  dramatic open-air opera stage. For the production of Aida, I was engaged to
  design a bold and vibrant visual identity that reflected the power of the
  performance, the coastal setting and the energy of the audience experience —
  immersive and rooted in the story of Aida, while highly functional in a public
  outdoor environment.
challenge: To create a cohesive and engaging visual world that echoed the
  grandeur of Aida while remaining approachable and contemporary for a diverse
  audience. The graphics needed to be high-impact from a distance, adaptable
  across formats, and able to withstand wind, salt and sun.
approach: Inspired by Aida's themes and costume palette, I developed a bold
  geometric design system using repeating diamond patterns and deep terracotta
  tones, contrasted with neutral stone textures. Custom Egyptian-inspired
  illustrations added character and theatricality to the space.
outcome: The final design created a striking and unified visual identity that
  anchored the event thematically and spatially. The strong graphic presence
  enhanced audience immersion, clearly articulated event flow, and provided a
  memorable backdrop that honoured the grandeur of Aida.
