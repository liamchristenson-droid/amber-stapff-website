overview: 'Sandbox Properties is a boutique developer driven by a vision to create lifestyle-oriented spaces that shape how people live and connect with their environment. Initially engaged to design the company''s website, I''ve since become a long-term creative partner, working across the full design lifecycle of their key residential developments: Marella, Ayre, Kyma and 200 Hedges.'
challenge: Each development needed to feel unique while staying aligned with Sandbox's broader values around design, scale and lifestyle. The challenge was to create high-impact identities that captured the spirit of each location — from beachfront calm to elevated luxury — and translate that across every medium.
approach: Every brand began with deep research into site context, target demographics and architectural tone. From there, I crafted tailored visual languages combining thoughtful typography, colour palettes and layouts to express each project's essence.
outcome: The result is a strong, consistent brand foundation for Sandbox Properties and its projects, where each development is instantly recognisable and deeply connected to its location and audience — supporting Sandbox's mission to develop sensibly scaled, lifestyle-driven spaces.
