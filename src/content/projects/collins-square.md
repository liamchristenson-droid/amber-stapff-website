overview: Collins Square is one of Australia's largest commercial precincts — a
  dynamic fusion of architecture, placemaking and premium office accommodation
  spanning an entire city block on Collins Street. I partnered with the Collins
  Square team to deliver a series of creative activations across the precinct,
  designed to engage tenants, elevate the visitor experience and reinforce the
  brand as a vibrant, people-centric destination.
challenge: Each activation required design solutions that would stand out in a
  high-traffic, architecturally rich environment while staying cohesive with the
  precinct's brand — from large-scale signage to digital content.
approach: My design response focused on clarity and brand alignment. Guided by
  campaign briefs, I developed scalable visual assets tailored to various
  precinct locations, with typography, colour and layout carefully refined to
  sit seamlessly within the existing visual language.
outcome: The designs brought each activation to life with clarity and cohesion,
  supporting precinct-wide marketing and engagement goals while reinforcing
  Collins Square's identity as a vibrant business hub and people-first
  destination.
