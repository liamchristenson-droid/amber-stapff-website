overview: Working under Rizer, I supported the graphic production of Audemars
  Piguet's Powerful Summer pop-up in Bangkok. The two-week activation brought
  the luxury watchmaker's global campaign into a physical retail environment,
  featuring bold architectural graphics, technical watch illustrations and
  branded displays. My role focused on adapting supplied campaign artwork into
  large-scale graphics and production-ready files for the installation.
challenge: The project involved translating existing global campaign assets into
  a range of physical formats, each with different dimensions, surfaces and
  production requirements. Maintaining the integrity of the original designs was
  essential, particularly when working with oversized graphics, intricate
  illustrations and the precise visual standards of a luxury brand.
approach: >-
  I worked with the supplied creative assets to develop artwork for the various
  elements throughout the pop-up, adapting layouts and preparing graphics to
  suit the architectural structures and display areas.

  This involved scaling artwork, resolving layouts against technical dimensions and preparing detailed print files for fabrication. Careful attention was given to artwork positioning, consistency and ensuring the supplied designs translated accurately from digital files into the finished physical environment.
outcome: The completed pop-up combined vibrant campaign graphics, detailed
  product imagery and architectural display elements to create a distinctive
  branded environment. My contribution supported the production and installation
  process, ensuring the campaign artwork was accurately adapted and prepared for
  its large-scale physical applications.
